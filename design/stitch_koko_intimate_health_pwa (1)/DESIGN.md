## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, deeply comforting, and radically non-judgmental digital refuge designed for young women and university students in Togo (West Africa). Navigating sexual, reproductive, and emotional health frequently carries intense social stigma, fear of exposure, and institutional alienation. The interface rejects sterile medical clinicalism and high-pressure tech aesthetics in favor of a private, warm physical space—evoking the safety of stepping behind an open, welcoming doorway ("Knock on the door of your intimate well-being").

The design personality is:
- **Discreet & Protective:** Visual indicators never scream sexual health; interactions favor confidentiality, quick exits, and visual discretion in shared or public spaces.
- **Warm & Organic:** Soft curves, gentle rounded doorways (arches), tactile earth-softened surfaces, and grounding botanical tones.
- **Empathetic & Direct:** Low cognitive load, bite-sized French guidance, and validating microcopy ("Tu peux tout demander ici", "Ici, personne ne te juge", "Anonyme & Confidentiel").

The stylistic foundation blends **Soft Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile/Tonal Layers**. Structural frames use gentle architectural curves resembling archways, while interactions remain whisper-quiet, calm, and forgiving.

## Colors

The color palette intentionally bans clinical whites and harsh pure blacks (`#000000`), using warm plant, sky, and earth tones to calm physiological stress and foster confidentiality.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Cream). Soft on eyes under bright outdoor sunlight or late-night room light.
  - Surface Container / Elevated Cards: `#F5EFEB` (Muted Warm Alabaster) and pure clean tint `#FFFFFF` used sparingly for nested input wells.
- **Primary (Sage):**
  - Anchor: `#698F74` (Deep Sage) provides WCAG AAA contrast against cream for key action states, links, and active indicators.
  - Soft Tint: `#A8C3B0` for active pill backdrops, quiet highlights, and decorative arch illustrations.
- **Secondary (Misty Lavender):**
  - Deep Lavender: `#8479A8` for reflective categories, mental health trackers, and quiet headers.
  - Soft Tint: `#C9C1E3` for gentle mood-tracking tags and soothing accents.
- **Accents & Highlights:**
  - Gentle Peach / Sand: `#F4D6C3` for physical wellness, warmth, and friendly highlights.
  - Calm Sky Blue: `#BFD8E6` for reassurance badges, water/cycle phases, and educational reading cards.
- **Ink / Typography:**
  - Primary Ink: `#3E4A4F` (Deep Soft Slate) ensures calm, high-contrast readability without harshness.
  - Secondary Ink: `#63737A` (Muted Slate) for helper text, timestamps, and subtle subtext.
- **Alert / SOS:**
  - Warm Terracotta: `#D98E73` replaces alarming medical crimson, preserving urgency while avoiding panic during emergency contraception advice or discreet help contacts.

## Typography

The type system blends the human, rounded warmth of **Quicksand** for display and headline levels with the structural clarity and high legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body and interaction controls.

- **Quicksand** is reserved for friendly greetings, section entry points, reassuring slogans, and affirmations. Its rounded terminals remove cold formality and communicate approachability.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** handles sensitive informational copy, long-form health Q&As, form fields, and discreet status labels. It maintains exceptional rendering performance on mid-tier mobile hardware and low-bandwidth connections typical of student user environments.
- All French microcopy maintains generous line heights to prevent visual fatigue, keeping sentences concise, rhythmically open, and non-intimidating.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a mobile-first, single-column focal stream that transitions to an adaptive fluid grid on wider viewpoints, prioritizing single-hand ergonomics and privacy.

- **Mobile Viewports (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Controls, question inputs, and navigation anchors are anchored to the bottom thumb-zone. Spacing between questions and sensitive sections uses `space-lg` to prevent accidental taps and reduce visual tension.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with centered reading rails. Maximum card width is capped at 680px for intimate Q&A threads and article narratives.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout capped at 1120px max content container, framed by generous margins to preserve an intimate, journal-like sanctuary rather than an expansive enterprise dashboard.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows and sharp borders, relying instead on **tonal nesting** and **ambient diffuse glows** tinted with sage and slate.

- **Level 0 (Sanctuary Base):** `#FAF7F2` canvas.
- **Level 1 (Quiet Containers & Content Cards):** `#F5EFEB` with no structural outline, or backed by a 1px border of `#EADFD3` (warm clay whisper border).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards / Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` background with an ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(62, 74, 79, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(105, 143, 116, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Privacy Veil, Emergency Quick-Hide):** Crisp flat-tint backdrop filters (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`), ensuring that any private content underneath is immediately obscured if a friend or peer glances at the screen.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary revolves around **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry, softened corners, and distinctive architectural arches.

- **The Archway Motif:** Key hero modules, question cards, and visual containers use asymmetric top-curved radii (`border-top-left-radius: 2.5rem; border-top-right-radius: 2.5rem; border-bottom-left-radius: 1rem; border-bottom-right-radius: 1rem`), directly echoing the welcoming, secure door arch of the platform's motto.
- **Interactive Badges & Buttons:** Strictly pill-shaped (`9999px`), creating gentle, stone-smoothed tactile buttons that invite effortless, unhurried touch.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button ("Continuer", "Poser ma question"):** Full pill-shaped surface filled with Deep Sage (`#698F74`), text in Warm Cream (`#FAF7F2`), using `label-lg`. Zero sharp edges. Height 48px to accommodate ergonomic thumb tapping.
- **Secondary Button ("En savoir plus"):** Pill border with Deep Sage tint (`#698F74`), background `#F5EFEB`, text `#3E4A4F`.
- **Ghost/Subtle Button:** Transparent surface, text in `#63737A`, subtle underline on hover/press.

### 2. Discreet Quick-Hide Button ("Bouton Discret / Sortie Rapide")
- A permanent, highly accessible floating pill situated in the upper-right corner or bottom sticky bar.
- Uses a neutral, camouflaged label such as *"Fermer / Calculatrice"* or an unobtrusive icon.
- Tapping instantly blanks the screen and navigates to a harmless educational page (e.g., standard French dictionary or general study notes), clearing visible session states for total physical discretion.

### 3. Pill Badges & Chips
- **Status & Topic Badges ("Anonyme & Confidentiel", "Cycle", "Bien-être"):** Height 28px–32px, rounded pill radius.
- Background uses soft tints: Soft Sage (`#A8C3B0` at 30% opacity), Lavender (`#C9C1E3` at 35% opacity), or Peach (`#F4D6C3` at 40% opacity).
- Text is set in `#3E4A4F` using `label-md`.

### 4. Input Fields & Confidential Prompts
- Form fields are generous (height 52px) with `rounded-xl` corners.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` inset in `#F5EFEB` cards, with a delicate 1px border `#E5DDD0`. Focus state smoothly transitions to a 1.5px border of `#698F74` with an ambient glow (`rgba(105, 143, 116, 0.15)`).
- Placeholder text in `#63737A` using gentle French prompts: *"Tape ta question en toute sécurité..."*.

### 5. Checkboxes, Radio Buttons & Mood Selectors
- Radio items and survey selectors are designed as large rounded cards or soft oval buttons instead of bare mechanical checkboxes.
- Active state fills the card with `#F5EFEB` accented by a Deep Sage `#698F74` inner checkmark and border.

### 6. Arch Cards ("Porte de Confiance")
- Signature card style: arched top (`rounded-t-[2.5rem] rounded-b-2xl`), background `#F5EFEB`.
- Includes a subtle reassurance header badge: *"Ici, personne ne te juge"* in `label-sm`.
- Used for community Q&As, daily wellness reflections, and educational guides.

### 7. SOS / Support Card
- Bordered in Warm Terracotta (`#D98E73` at 40% opacity) on a soft sand background (`#F4D6C3` at 20% opacity).
- Provides direct, confidential crisis line and local health contact details with zero stigmatizing terminology.
